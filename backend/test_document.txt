Photosynthesis is the process by which plants convert light energy into chemical energy.

Key Components:
1. Chlorophyll - The green pigment that captures light energy
2. Carbon Dioxide (CO2) - Absorbed from the air through stomata
3. Water (H2O) - Absorbed by roots from the soil
4. Sunlight - The energy source for the reaction

The Process:
In the chloroplasts, light energy is used to split water molecules, releasing oxygen as a byproduct. The energy is then used to combine carbon dioxide with hydrogen to form glucose (C6H12O6), which the plant uses for growth and energy storage.

Chemical Equation:
6CO2 + 6H2O + Light Energy → C6H12O6 + 6O2

This process occurs mainly in the leaves and is essential for life on Earth, as it produces the oxygen we breathe and forms the base of most food chains.
